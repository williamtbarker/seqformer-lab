# Changelog

All notable changes follow [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-03

### Added

- Condition-aware masked sequence transformer with tied token/output weights.
- Conditional transformer variational autoencoder and tested ELBO components.
- Deterministic synthetic sequence generator and strict JSONL interchange format.
- Expanding-window temporal folds and final-period holdout evaluation.
- Atomic model, configuration, metric, history, and provenance artifacts.
- CPU-runnable CLI demonstration and Linux/macOS continuous integration.
